Common questions

Stem separation is slow or heats up my device. Separation runs a neural model fully on-device; it's compute-heavy. Keep the app in the foreground until it finishes — leaving the app cancels it.

Online lyrics don't appear. Lyrics are fetched from a public database by title and artist; some songs simply aren't in it. Make sure the online lyrics option is enabled in settings and you're online.
